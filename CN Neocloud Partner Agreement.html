If a rate is left blank, the Upfront Fee is 1% and the Recurring Fee is 10%. Rates apply to amounts actually invoiced, exclusive of any sales, use or similar transaction taxes. A rate may only be changed by written agreement signed by both parties; a change applies to Introduced Clients registered after it is signed, and never reduces a fee already accrued.
  1. PurposeCN is a buyer-side advocate. It identifies and introduces prospective clients to the Provider and supports those relationships. CN does not buy, sell, resell or take title to anything. This Agreement records how CN is paid when an introduction turns into revenue.
  2. Introduced ClientAn Introduced Client is any person or entity that CN first brings to the Provider's attention under clause 3 and that the Provider does not validly exclude under clause 4. It includes that party's parent, subsidiaries and affiliates, and any successor to its business, in each case where the revenue arises from the same or a substantially similar requirement.
  3. Registration of an Introduction
    1. CN registers an introduction by written notice to the Provider naming the client and, where known, the contact and the requirement. Email is sufficient.
    2. Registration is effective on the date CN sends it, and creates a rebuttable presumption that CN introduced that client.
    3. An introduction is also taken to be registered where the Provider first learns of the client through CN by any means, and the parties then engage with that client.
  4. Pre-Existing Relationships ExcludedThis Agreement does not apply, and no fee is payable, where the Provider was already working with the client before the introduction. To rely on this, the Provider must object in writing within ten (10) business days of registration and produce documentary evidence — dated correspondence, a CRM record, a proposal or an executed agreement — showing an active discussion or existing relationship with that client concerning the same or a substantially similar requirement, in existence before the registration date. If the Provider does not object within that period with such evidence, the client is an Introduced Client. Deals already in progress before the introduction are outside this Agreement entirely.
  5. FeesThe Provider will pay CN the Upfront Fee and the Recurring Fee set out in Schedule A on all Revenue received from an Introduced Client. Revenue means all amounts invoiced by the Provider or any of its affiliates to an Introduced Client, including deposits, prepayments, committed-use amounts, monthly or other periodic charges, overage, expansion and renewal charges. Revenue excludes sales, use and similar transaction taxes and amounts genuinely refunded or credited.
  6. Fee Term — the Life of the RelationshipFees are payable for the full term of each agreement between the Provider and the Introduced Client, and of every renewal, extension, replacement or successor agreement with that client (the Fee Term). Fees continue where the client expands: additional capacity, additional products, additional sites, additional entities within the client's group, or a new agreement covering a substantially similar requirement. Termination of this Agreement under clause 12 does not end the Fee Term for a client already registered.

  1. Payment, Statements and Audit
    1. The Provider will pay each fee within fourteen (14) days of receiving the corresponding amount from the Introduced Client.
    2. With each payment the Provider will give CN a statement showing, per Introduced Client, the amounts invoiced and received in the period and the fee calculated.
    3. CN may, on 10 business days' notice and no more than twice a year, have an independent accountant review the Provider's records relevant to fees. CN bears the cost, unless the review finds an underpayment of more than 5%, in which case the Provider bears it and pays the shortfall.
    4. Amounts unpaid after 14 days carry interest at 1% per month, calculated daily.
  2. Partnership in PracticeCN intends to remain active in each Introduced Client relationship: supporting the client, advocating for their requirements, and working with the Provider to expand the account over time. The Provider will keep CN reasonably informed of the status of each Introduced Client, and will not exclude CN from the relationship while this Agreement is in effect.
  3. Non-CircumventionThe Provider will not, and will procure that its affiliates and personnel do not, structure, route, novate, assign or otherwise arrange any dealing with an Introduced Client — including through an affiliate, a joint venture, a reseller, an agent or a newly formed entity — with the purpose or effect of avoiding or reducing a fee payable under this Agreement. Any amount that would have been Revenue but for such an arrangement is treated as Revenue.
  4. If CN Goes Quiet
    1. If CN ceases all communication with an Introduced Client for a continuous period of twelve (12) months, the Provider may from the end of that period deal with that client directly without further involvement from CN.
    2. Clause 10(a) does not extinguish CN's fees. Where CN can show that it communicated with the Introduced Client — by email, meeting note, call record, message or other reasonable evidence — fees under this Agreement continue to accrue and remain payable in accordance with clause 6 for the Fee Term.
    3. A period during which CN is prevented from communicating by the client, by the Provider, or by circumstances outside CN's reasonable control does not count towards the twelve months.
    4. Before relying on clause 10(a) the Provider will give CN written notice and thirty (30) days to demonstrate communication.
  5. Change of Control and AssignmentNeither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor of substantially the whole of its business. Any successor to the Provider, and any acquirer of an Introduced Client contract, takes that contract subject to this Agreement, and the Provider will procure their written acknowledgment of it as a condition of the transaction.
  6. Term and TerminationThis Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until either party ends it on thirty (30) days' written notice. Termination does not affect: any Introduced Client registered before termination, the Fee Term for that client, any fee accrued, or clauses 4 to 11 and 13 to 18, which survive.
  7. ConfidentialityEach party will keep the other's confidential information, and the identity and details of Introduced Clients, confidential and use them only for this Agreement. Where the parties have signed a mutual non-disclosure and non-circumvention agreement, that agreement continues alongside this one; if they conflict on the subject of fees, this Agreement prevails.
  8. Independent Parties; No AuthorityThe parties are independent contractors. Nothing here creates a partnership at law, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither party may bind the other or hold itself out as able to do so. CN does not act as principal in any transaction between the Provider and a client.
  9. TaxesFees are exclusive of any sales, use or similar transaction taxes. Where such a tax applies to an amount payable under this Agreement, the paying party pays it on receipt of a valid invoice. Each party is responsible for its own income taxes.
  10. RecordsThe Provider will keep complete records of all Revenue from each Introduced Client for the Fee Term and for two years afterwards, sufficient to verify every fee payable.
  11. Remedies; Dispute ResolutionThe parties accept that damages alone may not adequately remedy a breach of clauses 6, 9 or 10, and that a party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief. Before starting proceedings (other than for urgent relief) a party will give written notice of the dispute, and senior representatives of both parties will meet in good faith within 14 days to try to resolve it.
  12. GeneralThis Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject matter and may be varied only in writing signed by both parties. If a provision is unenforceable it is severed to the minimum extent necessary. A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it. Notices must be in writing to the addresses on page 1 or as later notified; email is sufficient. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature. It is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, United States, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Delaware.
