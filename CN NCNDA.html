Mutual Non-Disclosure and Non-Circumvention Agreement

  1. PurposeThe parties wish to explore a possible commercial relationship concerning data center capacity, compute infrastructure and related services (the Purpose). To do so, each party may share information that is confidential to it.
  2. Confidential InformationConfidential Information means any non-public information disclosed by one party (the Discloser) to the other (the Recipient), in any form, relating to the Purpose. It includes technical, capacity, operational, commercial, pricing and customer information, the identity of counterparties introduced under this Agreement, and the existence and content of the parties' discussions. It does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement; (b) the Recipient already held without a duty of confidence; (c) the Recipient receives from a third party free to disclose it; or (d) the Recipient develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.
  3. Obligations of the RecipientThe Recipient will: (a) keep the Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the Purpose; (b) protect it with at least the care it applies to its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; and (c) not disclose it to anyone except its officers, employees, affiliates and professional advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations of confidence no less protective than these. The Recipient is responsible for any breach by a person to whom it discloses.
  4. Permitted DisclosureThe Recipient may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, a court, or a regulator. Where lawful to do so, it will give the Discloser prompt notice so the Discloser may seek protective measures, and will disclose only what is required.
  5. Non-CircumventionWhere one party introduces the other to a counterparty, contact or opportunity that the other did not already know of, the introduced party will not, for twelve (12) months from the date of that introduction, approach, negotiate with, or transact with that counterparty in relation to the Purpose while deliberately bypassing or excluding the introducing party, without the introducing party's prior written consent. This clause protects attribution and relationships only. It does not oblige either party to pay the other anything, and it does not apply to any counterparty the introduced party can show it already had a documented relationship or active discussion with before the introduction.
  6. No Obligation to TransactNothing in this Agreement obliges either party to disclose anything, to proceed with any transaction, to enter into any further agreement, or to refrain from pursuing any other opportunity. No exclusivity, agency, partnership, joint venture or employment relationship is created. Any transaction will be governed only by a separate written agreement signed by both parties.
  7. No AnnouncementNeither party will announce this Agreement, or use the other's name, logo or marks publicly, without the other's prior written consent.
  8. Return or DestructionOn the Discloser's written request, the Recipient will return or destroy the Confidential Information in its possession, and confirm it has done so. The Recipient may retain copies required by law or its ordinary backup and record-keeping systems, which remain subject to this Agreement.

  1. No Warranty; No Transfer of RightsConfidential Information is provided as is, without warranty as to accuracy or completeness. Nothing in this Agreement transfers or licenses any intellectual property or other right.
  2. TermThis Agreement begins on the Effective Date. Either party may end it on written notice; the obligations of confidentiality survive for twenty-four (24) months from the date of last disclosure, and the obligation in clause 5 survives for its stated period.
  3. RemediesThe parties accept that damages alone may not adequately remedy a breach of this Agreement, and that a party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy available to it.
  4. Entire Agreement; VariationThis Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and replaces any earlier understanding on it. It may be varied only in writing signed by both parties.
  5. AssignmentNeither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to a successor of substantially the whole of its business, which takes it subject to these terms.
  6. SeverabilityIf any provision is found unenforceable, it is severed to the minimum extent necessary and the rest of the Agreement continues in force.
  7. CounterpartsThis Agreement may be signed in counterparts, and by electronic signature or scanned copy, each of which is an original and together form one agreement.
  8. Governing LawThis Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, United States. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware.
