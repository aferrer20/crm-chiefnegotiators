A rate may only be changed by written agreement signed by both parties; a change applies to Introduced Customers registered after it is signed, and never reduces a fee already accrued.
  1. PurposeCN is a buyer-side advocate. It identifies compute buyers, introduces them to the Operator as prospective customers for uncontracted capacity, and supports those relationships as they scale. CN does not buy, sell, lease, resell or take title to capacity, power or equipment, and is not a broker of record for the Operator. This Agreement records how CN is paid when an introduction becomes contracted capacity.
  2. Introduced CustomerAn Introduced Customer is any person or entity that CN first brings to the Operator's attention under clause 3 and that the Operator does not validly exclude under clause 4. It includes that party's parent, subsidiaries and affiliates, any entity it nominates to contract in its place, and any successor to its business, in each case where the capacity arises from the same or a substantially similar requirement.
  3. Registration of an Introduction
    1. CN registers an introduction by written notice to the Operator naming the customer and, where known, the contact and the requirement. Email is sufficient.
    2. Registration is effective on the date CN sends it, and creates a rebuttable presumption that CN introduced that customer.
    3. An introduction is also taken to be registered where the Operator first learns of the customer through CN by any means, and the parties then engage with that customer.
  4. Pre-Existing Relationships ExcludedThis Agreement does not apply, and no fee is payable, where the Operator was already working with the customer before the introduction. To rely on this, the Operator must object in writing within ten (10) business days of registration and produce documentary evidence — dated correspondence, a CRM record, a tour log, an RFP response, a signed letter of intent or an executed agreement — showing an active discussion or existing relationship with that customer concerning the same or a substantially similar requirement, in existence before the registration date. If the Operator does not object within that period with such evidence, the customer is an Introduced Customer. Deals already in progress before the introduction are outside this Agreement entirely.
  5. FeesThe Operator will pay CN the Upfront Fee and the Recurring Fee set out in Schedule A on all Revenue received from an Introduced Customer. Revenue means all amounts invoiced by the Operator or any of its affiliates to an Introduced Customer under or in connection with a Capacity Agreement, including license, lease, space and rack charges; committed and metered power charges; connection, installation, fit-out, setup and cross-connect fees; remote hands and managed services; contractual escalations; overage and burst charges; and expansion, extension and renewal charges. Revenue excludes sales, use and similar transaction taxes, refundable security deposits until applied, amounts genuinely refunded or credited, and pass-through electricity charges billed at cost with no margin where the Operator evidences that treatment.
  6. Capacity AgreementCapacity Agreement means any colocation, license, lease, powered shell, build-to-suit, wholesale, retail or hosting agreement, or any other arrangement under which an Introduced Customer takes space, power or capacity from the Operator, at any site the Operator owns, operates, manages or has rights over, whether existing at the Effective Date or acquired or developed later.

  1. Fee Term — the Life of the RelationshipFees are payable for the full term of each Capacity Agreement with an Introduced Customer, and of every renewal, extension, replacement, novation or successor agreement with that customer (the Fee Term). Fees continue where the customer grows: additional megawatts, additional halls, cages, racks or suites, additional sites or campuses of the Operator, later phases or ramp increments under a staged schedule, additional entities within the customer's group, a move of the same requirement to a different site of the Operator, and a conversion of the arrangement from one form to another (for example colocation to powered shell, or license to lease). Termination of this Agreement under clause 13 does not end the Fee Term for a customer already registered.
  2. Payment, Statements and Audit
    1. The Operator will pay each fee within fourteen (14) days of receiving the corresponding amount from the Introduced Customer.
    2. With each payment the Operator will give CN a statement showing, per Introduced Customer: contracted capacity in MW, capacity energized and billing, the amounts invoiced and received in the period by category, and the fee calculated.
    3. CN may, on 10 business days' notice and no more than twice a year, have an independent accountant review the Operator's records relevant to fees, including capacity schedules and customer invoices. CN bears the cost, unless the review finds an underpayment of more than 5%, in which case the Operator bears it and pays the shortfall.
    4. Amounts unpaid after 14 days carry interest at 1% per month, calculated daily.
  3. Partnership in PracticeCN intends to remain active in each Introduced Customer relationship: advocating for the customer's requirements, supporting ramp and expansion, and working with the Operator to grow the account. The Operator will keep CN reasonably informed of the status of each Introduced Customer, including material changes to contracted capacity, and will not exclude CN from the relationship while this Agreement is in effect.
  4. Non-CircumventionThe Operator will not, and will procure that its affiliates, landlords, joint venture partners, agents, resellers and personnel do not, structure, route, novate, assign or otherwise arrange any dealing with an Introduced Customer — including through an affiliate, a special purpose vehicle, a joint venture, a reseller, a channel partner, a landlord or a newly formed entity — with the purpose or effect of avoiding or reducing a fee payable under this Agreement. Any amount that would have been Revenue but for such an arrangement is treated as Revenue.
  5. If CN Goes Quiet
    1. If CN ceases all communication with an Introduced Customer for a continuous period of twelve (12) months, the Operator may from the end of that period deal with that customer directly without further involvement from CN.
    2. Clause 11(a) does not extinguish CN's fees. Where CN can show that it communicated with the Introduced Customer — by email, meeting note, call record, site visit, message or other reasonable evidence — fees under this Agreement continue to accrue and remain payable in accordance with clause 7 for the Fee Term.
    3. A period during which CN is prevented from communicating by the customer, by the Operator, or by circumstances outside CN's reasonable control does not count towards the twelve months.
    4. Before relying on clause 11(a) the Operator will give CN written notice and thirty (30) days to demonstrate communication.
  6. Sale of a Site; Change of Control; AssignmentNeither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor of substantially the whole of its business. If the Operator sells, transfers, novates or grants rights over a site, a Capacity Agreement, or the entity holding either, to any person, that person takes subject to this Agreement in respect of every Introduced Customer at that site, and the Operator will procure their written acknowledgment of this Agreement as a condition of completing the transaction. If the Operator fails to do so, the Operator remains liable for the fees that would have been payable for the remainder of the Fee Term.
  7. Term and TerminationThis Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until either party ends it on thirty (30) days' written notice. Termination does not affect: any Introduced Customer registered before termination, the Fee Term for that customer, any fee accrued, or clauses 4 to 12 and 14 to 18, which survive.
  8. ConfidentialityEach party will keep the other's confidential information, and the identity and requirements of Introduced Customers, confidential and use them only for this Agreement. Where the parties have signed a mutual non-disclosure and non-circumvention agreement, that agreement continues alongside this one; if they conflict on the subject of fees, this Agreement prevails.
  9. Independent Parties; No AuthorityThe parties are independent contractors. Nothing here creates a partnership at law, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither party may bind the other or hold itself out as able to do so. CN does not act as principal, and gives no warranty to the Operator as to any customer's creditworthiness or performance.
  10. Taxes and RecordsFees are exclusive of any sales, use or similar transaction taxes; where such a tax applies, the paying party pays it on receipt of a valid invoice. Each party is responsible for its own income taxes. The Operator will keep complete records of all Revenue from each Introduced Customer for the Fee Term and for two years afterwards, sufficient to verify every fee payable.
  11. Remedies; Dispute ResolutionThe parties accept that damages alone may not adequately remedy a breach of clauses 7, 10, 11 or 12, and that a party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief. Before starting proceedings (other than for urgent relief) a party will give written notice of the dispute, and senior representatives of both parties will meet in good faith within 14 days to try to resolve it.
